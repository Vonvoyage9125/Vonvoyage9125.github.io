Wayfinder Privacy Policy

Wayfinder by Vonvoyage · Effective 27 October 2026

Short version: Wayfinder does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. Everything the app keeps stays on your phone.

Who we are

Wayfinder is a compass app with animated skins and short games, published on Google Play under the developer name Vonvoyage. In this policy "we" means the developer of Wayfinder.

Data we collect

None. Wayfinder has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not send any information about you or your device to us or to any third party.

Data kept on your phone

To work, the app saves a few things in its own private storage on your device:

  • your settings, such as the chosen skin and sound on/off
  • game progress: best scores, badges, streaks, collected items and your Stars balance
  • which skins you have unlocked

This data never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it permanently.

Sensors and permissions

  • Compass sensors (magnetometer, accelerometer, gyroscope) are read live on the device to show your heading and run the games. The readings are not saved or transmitted.
  • Vibration is used for haptic feedback.
  • Wayfinder does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Images you save or share

At the end of a game you can create a score card image. It is saved to your phone's Pictures/Wayfinder folder only when you tap Save or Share. If you tap Share, Android's share menu sends the image to the app you choose; what happens to it after that is governed by that app's privacy policy.

Purchases

Paid skins are bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never see your card, bank or other payment details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was made, so the app can unlock the skin. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Because Wayfinder collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change reaches users.
