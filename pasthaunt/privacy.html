Pasthaunt Privacy Policy

Pasthaunt by Vonvoyage (formerly Afterimage) · Effective 1 November 2026

Short version: Pasthaunt does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. Everything the game keeps stays on your phone.

Who we are

Pasthaunt is a snake game in which echoes of your past moves come back to chase you. It is published on Google Play under the developer name Vonvoyage. In this policy "we" means the developer of Pasthaunt.

Data we collect

None. Pasthaunt has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The game does not send any information about you or your device to us or to any third party. Scores, including Daily Echo scores, are kept only on your phone; there is no online leaderboard.

Data kept on your phone

To work, the game saves a few things in its own private storage on your device:

  • your settings, such as difficulty, look (light or dark), sound and music on/off, and the chosen skin
  • game progress: best scores, Daily Echo scores, badges, daily challenges, streaks and free-trial counts
  • which skins and extra lives you have unlocked or bought

This data never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it permanently.

Permissions

  • Vibration is used for short haptic feedback.
  • Internet and network state are used only by Google Play's billing library, to show prices and complete purchases. The game itself makes no network requests.
  • Pasthaunt does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Pictures you share

At the end of a run you can tap Share run to make a picture of your route. The picture is created in the game's private temporary storage and handed to Android's share menu, which sends it to the app you choose. What happens to it after that is governed by that app's privacy policy. The game does not save it to your gallery.

Purchases

Skins, extra lives and bundles are bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never see your card, bank or other payment details. The game only receives confirmation from Google Play of which items you own, so it can unlock them. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Refunds are handled by Google Play. You can request one at play.google.com; requests made within 48 hours of a purchase are usually handled automatically.

Children

Because Pasthaunt collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes how it handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change reaches players.
